Four counters, one queue each

Pay at the coupon window first, then collect from the counter.

  • Chai & snacks

    Chai, coffee, vada pav, samosa, bread pakora. Open all day and the fastest queue if you are short on time.

  • South Indian

    Idli, medu vada, three kinds of dosa. Batter is ground on site, so it closes once the batter is finished.

  • Thali counter

    One veg thali and one egg thali daily, served 12:00 to 3:00. Second helping of rice and dal is free.

  • Rolls & noodles

    Frankie rolls, hakka noodles, chilli paneer. Made to order, so expect a five minute wait at peak hours.

Paying with coupons

Buy coupon books of ₹100 or ₹500 from the window near the entrance. They do not expire, they work at every counter, and students with a valid ID card get one free chai with any thali.
